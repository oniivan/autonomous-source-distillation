# Packaged Context Packet

## Objective

Provide a reusable, proportionate methodology for distilling long, noisy, heterogeneous,
or evolving sources into source-faithful notes, auditable claims, useful synthesis, and a
recoverable handoff.

## Selected Patterns

- progressive disclosure: lean routing and invariants in `SKILL.md`, detailed schemas in
  `references/`, deterministic mechanics in `scripts/`;
- evidence ledger before synthesis;
- separate region, facet, risk, attribution, faithfulness, composition, representation,
  and utility checks;
- immutable source revisions and reciprocal claim supersession;
- separate structural validity, delivery readiness, and fresh-agent semantic proof;
- direct/light/serious routing keyed to risk and recovery need, with word counts only as
  defaults;
- continuous receipts for mutation, resource, semantic, and release verification.

## Adaptations

- Mechanical line/paragraph chunking is a fallback, not a universal semantic chunker.
- Query-focused relevance is a derived projection; source-faithful notes remain reusable.
- Deterministic validation checks declared contracts and references, not factual truth.
- Legacy schema-v2 bundles remain structurally auditable, while schema v3 owns readiness.

## Explicit Rejections

- No model router or numeric routing score before comparative evidence requires one.
- No general event-sourcing layer beyond immutable revisions and claim supersession.
- No scalar automatic factuality score as a release oracle.
- No counting overlap, mirrors, or derived sources as independent corroboration.
- No forced serious bundle for a stable 10-15 minute transcript with low risk and no
  resume/audit requirement.

## Quality Gates

1. Starter bundle passes schema-v3 structure and readiness.
2. Malformed and false-green mutation matrix returns machine-readable expected outcomes.
3. Chunk output parity and representative resource ceilings pass.
4. Fresh-agent cases C, D, G, and I pass a withheld semantic oracle; D is order-invariant.
5. Tests pass in a randomly named external checkout.
6. Installed and exported copies match the corrective source state.

## Canonical Evidence

Research sources and native locators are in `citation-registry.md`. Design implications
are in `evidence-ledger.md`; atomic methodology claims are in `claim-ledger.md`.
Corrective execution evidence is in `implementation-receipt.md`.
